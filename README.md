# Peeko, Pengo and Axolo

Little desktop pets for your Mac that plan your day with you.

This repository only holds the finished apps and their update feeds. The apps check here for new versions and update themselves.

## Download

Get the newest version under [Releases](https://github.com/oblivixn-studios/pet-apps/releases/latest):

- **Peeko** (ghost): `Peeko-x.y.z.dmg`
- **Pengo** (penguin): `Pengo-x.y.z.dmg`
- **Axolo** (axolotl): `Axolo-x.y.z.dmg`

Open the DMG and drag the app into Applications. Needs macOS 26 or newer.

Updates arrive by themselves: the app checks once a day and asks before installing. Your tasks and settings stay as they are.
